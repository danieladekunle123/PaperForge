What we heard

The brief

A desktop PDF toolkit for Windows whose standout feature is Document Assembly: drop in PDFs and images, pick pages from any of them, and export one new document. Everything stays on your computer. It should feel fast, clear and trustworthy, like a well-made tool.

fastcleartrustworthylocalwell-made

Success: distinct from red PDF badges and plain dog-eared pages, clear on a 24 px Windows taskbar, works in one colour, and fits the app's dark interface and amber accent.

Final design

The Weld

Two sheets interlock around a new page, the hot spot where they fuse: Document Assembly as forge-welding.

  • Draws the product's standout feature instead of a generic document
  • Two identical sheets, rotated 180 degrees, give it a steady, engineered balance
  • Amber is already the app's accent colour, and here it means heat
  • Three drawings keep it sharp at 16 px, at 24 px and at full size
